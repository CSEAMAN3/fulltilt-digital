When someone searches for a service you offer, your business should show up.

If it doesn’t, you’re losing enquiries to competitors who are easier to find.

That’s where local SEO comes in.

Local SEO is what helps your business appear in search results when people are looking for services **in your area**.

And for most local service businesses, it’s one of the most valuable sources of leads.

Here’s how it works — and how to improve it.

---

## What Is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of improving your online presence so your business appears in:

- Google search results
- Google Maps
- Local listings

Especially for searches like:

- “plumber near me”
- “roofing company Norwich”
- “drainage services in [location]”

If your business isn’t showing up for these, you’re missing high-intent customers.

And even if you _are_ getting traffic, it doesn’t mean you’re converting it — which is where this guide on [getting more enquiries from your website](/blog/get-more-enquiries-from-your-website) comes in.

---

## 1. Your Google Business Profile Matters More Than You Think

Your Google Business Profile is one of the biggest factors in local SEO.

It’s what appears in map results and local listings.

### What to focus on:

- Accurate business name, address and phone number
- Clear service categories
- High-quality photos
- Regular updates and posts
- Customer reviews

The more complete and active your profile is, the better it performs.

---

## 2. Location-Specific Website Content

Google needs to understand:

- What you do
- Where you do it

If your website doesn’t clearly mention your location, it’s harder to rank locally.

### What this looks like:

- Service pages that include locations
- Dedicated landing pages for key areas
- Natural mentions of towns and service areas

For example:

> “Drainage services in Norwich and surrounding areas”

This helps search engines connect your business to local searches.

---

## 3. Consistent Business Information (NAP)

NAP stands for:

- Name
- Address
- Phone number

Your business details should be consistent across:

- Your website
- Google Business Profile
- Directories

Inconsistencies can confuse search engines and reduce trust.

---

## 4. Reviews Build Visibility and Trust

Reviews don’t just influence customers — they influence rankings.

Businesses with more high-quality reviews tend to perform better in local search.

### What to do:

- Ask customers for reviews
- Respond to every review
- Keep them coming in consistently

This builds both credibility and visibility.

Trust plays a huge role in whether someone chooses your business — something we explore further in [what makes a high-converting website](/blog/high-converting-websites-for-local-businesses).

---

## 5. Technical SEO Still Matters

Even for local businesses, the technical side of your website plays a role.

If your site is slow, poorly structured or hard to crawl, it will struggle to rank.

### Key areas:

- Page speed
- Mobile performance
- Clean site structure
- Proper use of headings

These foundations are part of effective [SEO services](/services/seo) and make a real difference over time.

---

## 6. Internal Linking Helps Everything Work Together

Your website shouldn’t be a collection of isolated pages.

Everything should connect.

### What this looks like:

- Linking between related services
- Connecting blog posts to service pages
- Building a clear site structure

This helps both users and search engines understand your site better.

It’s also one of the most overlooked SEO wins — and something we’re actively doing across these blog posts.

---

## 7. Content Builds Long-Term Visibility

Blog content allows you to target:

- Questions
- Problems
- Search terms your customers are using

For example:

- “How to fix blocked drains”
- “How much does a new roof cost”

This brings in traffic and builds authority over time.

If you’re not creating content yet, you’re relying entirely on your main service pages — which limits your growth potential.

---

## Why Local SEO Is So Valuable

Unlike social media or ads, local SEO targets people who are already looking for your services.

That means:

- Higher intent
- Better quality leads
- More consistent enquiries

Done properly, it becomes one of the most reliable ways to grow your business.
