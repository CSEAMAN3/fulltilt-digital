If your website isn’t generating enquiries, there’s usually a reason.

In most cases, it’s not because your service isn’t good — it’s because your website isn’t doing its job properly.

Small issues in structure, messaging or performance can have a big impact on how many people actually get in touch.

Here are five of the most common mistakes — and how to fix them.

---

## 1. Unclear Messaging

If people can’t quickly understand what you do, they won’t stay.

Vague headlines, generic wording and unclear positioning create confusion — and confused visitors don’t enquire.

### Fix it:

- Clearly state your service
- Mention your location
- Keep your messaging simple and direct

Your website should answer the question:

> “Is this the right business for me?” — immediately.

If it doesn’t, you’re losing people before they even consider contacting you.

This is one of the biggest reasons websites fail to convert — something we break down in more detail in [what makes a high-converting website](/blog/high-converting-websites-for-local-businesses).

---

## 2. No Clear Call-to-Action

A surprising number of websites don’t actually ask users to take action.

Or they hide the call-to-action so well that it gets missed.

### Fix it:

Use clear, consistent CTAs like:

- “Get a quote”
- “Book a call”
- “Start your enquiry”

Place them:

- At the top of the page
- Throughout key sections
- At the end of every page

If users don’t know what to do next, they won’t do anything.

Guiding users clearly is a key part of turning visitors into leads. If your site isn’t doing that, this guide on [getting more enquiries from your website](/blog/get-more-enquiries-from-your-website) explains what to change.

## 3. Slow Load Times

Speed matters more than most people realise.

A slow website creates friction, increases bounce rates and reduces trust.

Most users won’t wait around for a page to load — especially on mobile.

### Fix it:

- Optimise images
- Avoid heavy, bloated templates
- Use modern, performance-focused builds

Speed is also a key part of search visibility. If your site is slow, it can impact how well you rank — something we address through our [SEO services](/services/seo).

## 4. Poor Mobile Experience

Most of your visitors are on their phones.

If your website doesn’t work properly on mobile, you’re losing a large percentage of potential enquiries.

### Fix it:

- Use responsive layouts
- Make text easy to read
- Ensure buttons are easy to tap
- Keep navigation simple

Your website should feel just as easy to use on mobile as it does on desktop.

---

## 5. Weak or Missing Trust Signals

People won’t contact a business they’re unsure about.

If your website doesn’t build confidence, visitors will leave and choose someone else.

### Fix it:

Add:

- Customer testimonials
- Photos of your work
- Clear service descriptions
- Location-specific content

Strong branding also plays a major role here.

A consistent, professional visual identity helps build trust quickly — something we explore in [why branding matters for local businesses](/blog/why-branding-matters-for-local-businesses).

---

## The Real Problem

Most websites aren’t built with performance in mind.

They’re designed to look good — not to generate enquiries.

That’s why they don’t deliver results.

A high-performing website is built around:

- Clarity
- Trust
- Structure
- Speed
- Conversion

Everything has a purpose.
